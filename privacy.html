Afterbloom Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: We do not collect, share or sell any personal information, and the Game has no accounts. Your progress is saved only on your own device. The Android and iPhone versions offer one ad from Google AdMob after you beat the last level, which you can skip, and the web version on CrazyGames shows ads. Those ad companies may collect information under their own privacy policies.

Who we are

Afterbloom (the "Game") is made by Washed Gamer Studios ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email washedgamerstudios@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information, including your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.

In every version of the Game:

  • There are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • Coins, wood and items in the Game are in-game rewards only. The Game has no purchases with real money.

The phone versions (Google Play and the Apple App Store) and the web version (CrazyGames) show ads provided by other companies. See "Ads in the phone versions" and "Web version on CrazyGames" below. Apart from these ads, the Game has no analytics or tracking.

Ads in the phone versions

The Android and iPhone versions offer one full-screen ad after you beat the last level, provided by Google AdMob. Before it plays, the Game asks whether you want to watch it or skip it. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the Game may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you interact with ads, and information about the app's and the ad software's performance.

This information goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the AD PRIVACY CHOICES button in Settings > Credits & Legal. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). On iPhone, the Game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it cannot use your device's advertising identifier.

Web version on CrazyGames

A web version of the Game is published on CrazyGames, which hosts it and shows ads in it. In the web version:

  • A short ad may play between levels, when you continue from a results screen. CrazyGames decides when ads play and limits how often.
  • After a level you can choose to watch an ad to double the coins you earned. This is optional, and the coins are in-game rewards only.
  • If you are signed in to CrazyGames, your game progress is saved to your CrazyGames account so it follows you between devices. If you are not signed in, it is saved in your browser.
  • CrazyGames and its advertising partners may collect information such as your device, browser, approximate location and ad interactions, and may use cookies, under CrazyGames' own privacy policy. We do not receive this information. See CrazyGames' privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

In the phone versions, your game progress and settings (levels reached, stars, coins, wood, gear, sound and vibration settings) are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. We never receive this information. Deleting the Game deletes it.

Device permissions

On Android, the Game may use your phone's vibration for a short buzz when your character is hit. You can turn this off in the Game's settings. The Android and iPhone versions use internet access only for the ad; on Android, the ad also uses the advertising ID. The Game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

App stores

You download the Game from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about you and your device under their own privacy policies, for example your purchase history or, if you have agreed to share it, crash and performance reports. Those reports reach us only as summaries that do not identify you. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Privacy laws in places such as the European Union, the United Kingdom and California give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information a company holds about you. We do not hold any personal information about you. For information collected by Google AdMob or CrazyGames, use the choices above or contact those companies under their privacy policies. You can contact us at any time at washedgamerstudios@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if ads or online features are added or changed, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version of the Game is released.
